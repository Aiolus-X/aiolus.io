FLEX

Forward Learning from Experience

Project homepage ↗项目主页 ↗

FLEX introduces a new learning paradigm for AI, fundamentally overturning the traditional model-training framework based on gradient backpropagation. It enables agents to continuously self-evolve through experience, taking a step toward artificial general intelligence (AGI). FLEX 是 AI 全新学习范式，彻底颠覆了依赖梯度反向传播的传统模型训练框架，让智能体通过“经验”进行持续的自我进化，朝着通用人工智能（AGI）迈进了一步。

Continuous self-evolution during deployment. 在部署中持续自进化。

Knowledge transfer across agents to build collective intelligence. 智能体之间的知识迁移，构建群体智能。
